Itertone: Synth Workshop — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-19

In short: this app collects no personal data. It has no server, no user account and no sign-in. Your game progress, saved patches and exported audio stay in the app's data area on your device.

What data the app handles, and where it stays

Itertone: Synth Workshop stores your game progress (stars, scores, unlocked modules, Daily Jam streak), your saved patches, your settings and any WAV files you export in the app's protected data area on your device. You can remove saved patches in the app, and all of it by deleting the app. Exported WAV files go only where you choose to save or share them.

The app has no server component and makes no network connections of its own; the only network use is Apple's StoreKit for the in-app purchase. It contains no code that transmits your progress or patches anywhere. The provider has no access to them at any time.

No analytics and no tracking

The app integrates no analytics, crash-reporting, attribution or tracking components and uses no third-party SDK. It builds no usage profile, reads no device or advertising identifier, and never shows an App Tracking Transparency request.

Purchases

The app offers one in-app purchase, the one-time "Full Workshop" unlock. The purchase and the payment are handled entirely by the platform (Apple's App Store or Google Play). The provider receives no payment details, no card data and no account identifier — only whether a valid purchase exists for this device.

Legal basis and retention

Because the provider neither collects nor processes personal data, no processing by the provider takes place within the meaning of the GDPR. The data you enter remains on your device until you delete it or remove the app.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Because the provider holds no data about you, there is nothing it can disclose or erase on request — control over your data rests directly with you, on your device.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 16, and the provider does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's functionality changes in a way that matters for data protection, this policy is updated before that version is released.

Contact
